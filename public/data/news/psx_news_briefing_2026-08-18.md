## Macro, policy, and regulation

**State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)**
The SBP has comprehensively reviewed and updated the Prudential Regulations for Housing Finance. Key changes include:
- Setting the maximum tenor for housing finance at 30 years.
- Setting the maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio at 90:10.
- Capping total monthly amortization payments (including other consumer-financing obligations) at 65% of the borrower's net disposable income.
- Mandating property assessment by a PBA-panel valuator for financing above Rs10 million.
- Requiring banks/DFIs to obtain comprehensive insurance/takaful coverage of the financed housing unit.

**Finance Ministry**The government has directed the digitalisation of all government-to-person (G2P) payments to eliminate the use of physical cheques. The government also seeks to expand the digital banking ecosystem through partnerships like Z-Wallet.**Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP)**The SECP has issued a "Practical Guide and Toolkit for Corporate Sukuk Issuance" to standardize Sukuk structures and documentation.
