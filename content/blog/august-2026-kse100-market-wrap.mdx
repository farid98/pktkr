- **KSE-100:** +0.50%, or +881.56 points, to 176,975.67.

- **Leadership:** exploration +5.08%; refiners +31.79%, but only 1.28% of basket value.

## The market gain was led by a small set of large companies

The index rose 0.50%; the market-cap-weighted basket rose 0.54%, versus +0.33% equal-weight. August was therefore a selective advance, not a broad rally.

The contrast between percentage returns and index influence is the important part of this month’s story. A stock can have an exceptional rally without moving the overall market much if its market-cap weight is small; conversely, a more modest move in a heavyweight can materially affect the index.

- <Ticker symbol="OGDC" /> gained **4.67%**. With an 8.51% end-August share of this market-cap basket, that equates to roughly **+0.40 percentage points** of the basket’s monthly return—the largest positive contribution.
- <Ticker symbol="PPL" /> rose **8.46%** and represented 3.84% of the basket, adding about **+0.33 percentage points**.
- <Ticker symbol="CNERGY" /> was the headline percentage winner, up **43.95%**, but its 0.51% weight meant its estimated impact was closer to **+0.23 percentage points**.
- <Ticker symbol="ATRL" /> advanced **23.69%**. Its 0.77% weight translated to about **+0.18 percentage points** of contribution.

Together, the four names supplied roughly **+1.13 percentage points** of positive return before the rest of the basket—particularly weaker large sectors—offset part of that advance. This is why August finished marginally higher at index level even though more constituents fell than rose.

## What to watch in September

1. **Whether breadth catches up with the index.** A second positive month with more than 44 advancing constituents would be a stronger confirmation than index gains driven by a handful of large companies.
2. **Banks and fertiliser.** Together they represented 37.86% of this market-cap basket at month-end. Their modest August weakness matters more to the index than the performance of many small sectors.
3. **Whether the refinery move broadens or remains concentrated.** Refinery returns were exceptional, but the sector’s small weight limits its index effect unless the rally spreads to larger parts of the market.

## Caveats

This is an analytical view of the 100 companies in pktkr’s KSE-100 daily CSVs, not an official index attribution. Market-cap-weighted sector and basket returns use each company’s total end-August market capitalisation, whereas the official KSE-100 uses its own methodology and free-float weights. Closing prices and returns are not adjusted for dividends. The <Ticker symbol="SRVI" /> starting price is adjusted for its 10-for-1 share split; without that adjustment, its price change would be mechanically misleading.
