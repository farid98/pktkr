Pakistan’s four-wheeler sales rose **19% year on year to 50,480 units in July–September 2026**. But September’s slowdown shows the recovery is still uneven.

Passenger cars led quarterly growth, rising 43%, while light commercial vehicles, vans and jeeps fell 34%. Motorcycles, tractors and trucks provided additional support.

## September was weaker than it looked

Reported four-wheeler sales fell 12% year on year and just 3% from August to 15,104 units. There’s a catch: **Sazgar’s September submission included two months of sales**, while August excluded its pending figures.

Across manufacturers excluding Sazgar, September sales fell approximately **19% month on month and 20% year on year**. The headline therefore understates the weakness in that group.

## The company split matters

- **Indus Motor:** September sales rose 31% year on year to 4,142 units, maintaining momentum.
- **Pak Suzuki:** Sales fell 46% to 4,862 units. Ravi’s discontinuation explains part of the decline; excluding Ravi from last year’s comparison, sales still dropped approximately 33%.
- **Honda Atlas:** Sales slipped 8% to 2,128 units, despite 52% quarterly growth.
- **Sazgar:** Quarterly Haval/Tank volumes declined 12%. Its combined August–September submission prevents a clean September comparison.

## Beyond cars, growth held up

Quarterly two-wheeler sales increased 29%, tractors 38% and trucks 53%. Tractor momentum deserves qualification: PAMA links September’s sharp monthly rebound to the Punjab Green Tractor Scheme. [PAMA commentary](https://pama.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Write-up-for-September-2026.pdf%20)

**The sector is recovering, but the headline is stronger than the finish.** The next test is whether monthly growth broadens across assemblers—and whether higher volumes translate into stronger margins. Policy clarity remains important; PAMA continues to flag hesitation among manufacturers and buyers.

*Sources: Arif Habib Limited’s 1QFY27 auto-sales summary and [PAMA’s September 2026 release](https://pama.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Production-Sales-September-2026.pdf%20). Four-wheelers comprise cars, LCVs, vans, jeeps and separately reported EVs within the reporting dataset.*
