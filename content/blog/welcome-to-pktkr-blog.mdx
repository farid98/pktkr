This is the new home for **company deep dives**, market notes, and Pakistan economy analysis. Each article is a version-controlled MDX file, so its text, tables, and diagrams travel with the site.

## What we will publish

| Format | What it covers | Typical evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company deep dive | Business model, results, valuation context, and risks | Filings, annual reports, PSX disclosures |
| Market note | A focused development from the latest session | Market-close data and announcements |
| Economy brief | Trade, exports, inflation, policy, and macro trends | Official datasets and primary releases |

## How a post is made

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Write an MDX file] --> B[Add frontmatter]
  B --> C[Push to the repository]
  C --> D[pktkr publishes the post]
```

The economy news feed can move into this section later as tagged posts—for example, **Economy**, **Company news**, or **Market note**—without changing the publishing workflow.

## A note on methodology

Articles will distinguish reported facts from interpretation, link to primary sources where possible, and state material uncertainty. They are for information and discussion, not investment recommendations.
