## Market backdrop

The KSE-100 index experienced a sharp rally on Monday, jumping over 5,300 points (3.14%) to reach 176,391.90 [ Express Tribune Business](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2620545/psx-roars-back-as-us-iran-tensions-ease).

**Causal explanations stated by sources:**
- The rally was driven by "easing geopolitical tensions" and a "sharp decline in global oil prices" [ Dawn Business](https://www.dawn.com/news/2018715/psx-opens-week-in-the-green-gains-more-than-7000-points-during-intraday-trade).
- Investors reacted to the "pause in hostilities" between the US and Iran, which lifted risk appetite [ Express Tribune Business](https://trib-news.com/story/2620545/psx-roars-back-as-us-iran-tensions-ease).

**Researcher inference:**
- The market appears to be rewarding a "mean-reversion" on oil prices, which had surged recently due to Middle East conflict.
- There is a clear shift toward risk-on sentiment as the immediate threat of further escalation receded, providing breathing space for domestic equities.
