## How each metric is calculated

- **Daily percentage change:** the CSV's `percent_change` field, representing the share-price move from the last-day closing price (LDCP) to the current close.
- **Top gainers:** the five stocks with the highest daily percentage change. This ranks percentage price performance, not the rupee change in price.
- **Top losers:** the five stocks with the lowest, most negative daily percentage change.
- **Advances / declines / unchanged:** counts of stocks with positive, negative, or zero daily percentage change.
- **Advance–decline ratio:** number of advancing stocks divided by number of declining stocks.
- **Equal-weight average move:** arithmetic mean of all 100 stocks' daily percentage changes; every company has equal influence.
- **Median stock move:** the middle daily percentage change after sorting all 100 stocks, reducing the influence of extreme movers.
- **Market-cap-weighted basket move:** sum of each stock's daily percentage change multiplied by its share of combined market capitalisation.
- **Total reported volume:** sum of reported share volume across all 100 companies.
- **Volume leaders:** the five stocks with the highest reported number of shares traded.
- **Estimated traded value:** closing price multiplied by reported volume. This is an approximation and is not official turnover.
- **Estimated traded-value leaders:** the five stocks with the highest estimated `close × volume` value.
- **Combined market capitalisation:** sum of the market-cap values for all 100 companies.
- **Intraday range:** `(high - low) / LDCP × 100`; the widest-range table ranks the five largest results.
- **Basket weight:** a company's market capitalisation divided by the combined market capitalisation of all 100 companies.
- **Weighted contribution:** daily percentage change multiplied by basket weight, expressed in percentage points.
- **Sector weighted move:** market-cap-weighted average daily percentage change of companies within that sector.
- **Sector market-cap share:** sector market capitalisation divided by combined market capitalisation.
- **Sector A/D/U:** counts of advancing, declining, and unchanged companies in the sector.

## Limitations

- Market-cap-weighted basket and contribution figures use total company market capitalisation, not official KSE-100 free-float index weights. They are analytical estimates rather than the official index return or point contribution.
- Closing price multiplied by volume does not account for the prices of individual trades during the session.
