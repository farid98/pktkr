# PSX News Briefing — 2026-07-28

## Executive summary

* **Pakistan's energy security bolstered by refinery policy amendments and strategic reserve mandate**: Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif chaired a Cabinet Committee on Energy (CCoE) meeting where amendments to the Pakistan Oil Refining Policy 2023 were approved. The reforms aim to modernize existing refineries to produce Euro-V compliant fuels, reduce furnace oil output, and improve air quality. Additionally, the PM directed authorities to increase strategic petroleum reserves and launch roadshows in Gulf countries to attract investment for brownfield refinery projects.

  ==green::Positive==: Long-term energy security improvements and potential reduction in fuel import dependency may favor domestic refining sector investments.

  **Source:** [Dawn Business](https://www.dawn.com/news/2018968/pm-orders-increase-in-countrys-strategic-petroleum-reserves-changes-to-oil-refining-policy-approved)
  **Source:** [Mettis Global](https://mettisglobal.news/PM-greenlights-refinery-policy-amendments-seeks-Gulf-funding-62194)

* **PSX sees robust activity amid shifting geopolitical dynamics**: The Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) saw a significant rebound on Monday, with the KSE-100 Index jumping over 5,300 points as investors reacted to de-escalation between the US and Iran. While some sectors faced pressure due to tech sector concerns in Asia, key domestic sectors including cement, banking, and oil/gas exploration saw strong buying interest.

  ==green::Positive==: Investor sentiment remains sensitive to Middle East tensions; de-escalation provides immediate relief for local equity markets.

  **Source:** [Express Tribune Business](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2620545/psx-roars-back-as-us-iran-tensions-ease)

* **SBP maintains policy rate at 11.5% despite mixed economic signals**: The State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) decided to keep the policy rate unchanged at 11.5%. While headline inflation moderated in June, core pressures and external risks from the Middle East conflict led to a cautious stance. SBP noted improvements in foreign exchange reserves and sovereign credit ratings while acknowledging that economic activity remains susceptible to global commodity price volatility.

  ==blue::Neutral==: Monetary policy remains restrictive; focus shifts to managing inflationary pressures amid external shocks.

  **Source:** [Dawn Business](https://www.dawn.com/news/2018718/central-bank-keeps-policy-rate-unchanged-at-115pc)
  **Source:** [Mettis Global](https://mettisglobal.news/SBP-keeps-policy-rate-unchanged-at-62152)
